Color Psychology

Color psychology is the study of how different hues, shades, and visual light wavelengths affect human mood, behavior, and physiological responses.

Why Does Color Matter?

Colors matter to customers because they trigger instant emotional reactions and shape subconscious judgments about a brand before a person even reads any text.

Colors and their effects

orange

boosts energy and playfulness while signaling affordability to value-seeking shoppers.

Examples

  • Emotional Effects: Evokes enthusiasm, warmth, playfulness, and high energy.
  • Physical Effects: Increases oxygen supply to the brain and stimulates mental activity.
  • Customer Behavior: Signals affordability and value while remaining approachable and friendly.

red

creates instant urgency and stimulates appetite by raising your physical heart rate.

Examples

  • Emotional Effects: Triggers passion, excitement, urgency, and survival instincts.
  • Physical Effects: Raises heart rates, elevates blood pressure, and stimulates appetite.
  • Customer Behavior: Drives impulse buying, creates a sense of scarcity, and commands immediate attention.

yellow

sparks immediate optimism and warmth but can cause visual fatigue if overused.

Examples

  • Emotional Effects: Sparks happiness, positivity, intellect, and creative energy.
  • Physical Effects: Stimulates the nervous system and creates visual friction that demands attention.
  • Customer Behavior: Catches the eye quickly from a distance but can cause visual fatigue if overused.

pink

provides calming comfort and empathy, which helps soften a buyer's defensive guard.

Examples

  • emotional Effects: Radiates romance, gentleness, empathy, and calming comfort.
